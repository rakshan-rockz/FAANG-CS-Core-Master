# Spaced Review Queue

When a concept first reaches 🟨, add a row with reviews due at **+2, +7, +21, +60 days**. `/today` runs due
items (≤ 10 min) before new material. A review = cold derive-back from the Hook in ≤ 5 min + one Probe.
Mark a date cell `✅ YYYY-MM-DD` or `❌ YYYY-MM-DD` when done; a ❌ resets that concept to +2 days (new row)
and opens a weak area. Unmarked dates ≤ today are due. (Rungs H2–H3 at learn time move the first review to +1.)

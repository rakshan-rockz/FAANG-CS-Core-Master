# Phase 15 — Interview Mastery

Synthesis and performance. Everything from Phases 0–14 is now retrieved cold, at interview speed, in the
formats the target companies actually use: rapid-fire fundamentals rounds (OS, DBMS, CN, OOP), timed SQL
rounds, "trace the whole stack" deep questions, résumé-driven crypto/PQC/QKD grilling, company-styled vivas,
and the sealed baseline redone under identical conditions to measure the whole course's growth.
**Xref:** every phase; SD's interview-mastery phase (SD Phase 11) covers system-design rounds — this phase is
the fundamentals/SQL/theory counterpart. The learner's `reference/interview-bank.md` (built all along) is the
study set and is audited here.
**Reading:** none scheduled; this phase is retrieval and performance, not new material.

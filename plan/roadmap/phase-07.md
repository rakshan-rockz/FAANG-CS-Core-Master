# Phase 7 — OS: Persistence & Linux Internals

OSTEP chapters 36–45 and 48–49. From the device register up: interrupts and DMA, hard disks and SSDs (and
why SSDs are secretly log-structured), RAID, file-system implementation, crash consistency and journaling,
log-structured file systems, data integrity and the real meaning of `fsync`; then the Linux I/O stack,
epoll/io_uring internals, containers from kernel primitives, and virtualisation.
**Xref:** SD 0.3.2 (page cache & fsync cost at design level), SD 2.3.1 (WAL, pages at design level),
SD 2.9 (object/block/file storage and GFS-style systems), SD 8.6.1 (containers: namespaces/cgroups at design
level). Phase 11 builds WAL/ARIES on top of 7.2.4–7.2.6; 11.2.5 LSM trees connect to 7.2.5 LFS and 7.1.3 FTLs.
**Reading:** OSTEP 36–45, 48–49, Appendix B; McKusick 1984; Rosenblum & Ousterhout 1992; Pillai 2014;
Axboe 2019; Bovet & Cesati *Understanding the Linux Kernel* 3e ch 12, 14.

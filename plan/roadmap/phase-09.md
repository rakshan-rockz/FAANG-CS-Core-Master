# Phase 9 — Networks II: Network & Link Layers

Kurose & Ross chapters 4–7 plus *TCP/IP Illustrated* for IPv4, ICMP, DHCP, ARP and NAT. The data plane
(IP datagrams, addressing and subnetting, router internals, NAT, IPv6), the control plane (link-state and
distance-vector routing, OSPF, BGP), the link layer (error detection, multiple access, Ethernet, ARP, switches,
VLANs, data-centre fabrics), wireless basics, and a full packet-level trace with real tools.
**SD overlap map:** SD 1.1.1 names IP, CIDR, NAT and firewalls at design level and SD 8.7.1 covers cloud VPCs
and subnets as a user of them; SD 1.3.3 anycast/GSLB. This phase goes to how a router forwards, how routes are
computed, how an address is split, and what every header on the wire contains. Subnetting and "a day in the
life of a web request" are frequent fundamentals-round questions.
**Reading:** K&R 4–7; TCPIP 2–8.

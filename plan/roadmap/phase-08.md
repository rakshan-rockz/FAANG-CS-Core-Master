# Phase 8 — Networks I: Application & Transport

Kurose & Ross chapters 1–3 (top-down) plus *TCP/IP Illustrated* Vol 1 for the wire-level truth. Layering and
delays, HTTP and DNS as bytes on the wire, the sockets API with real servers, reliable-transfer theory
derived from scratch, TCP connection management, reliability, flow and congestion control, and QUIC.
**SD overlap map:** SD 1.1.1 (layers, ports, NAT at design level), 1.1.2–1.1.3 (DNS resolution & DNS in
architecture), 1.1.4–1.1.5 (TCP handshake, TIME_WAIT, flow/congestion, BBR, Nagle), 1.1.6 (TCP vs UDP),
1.2.1–1.2.2 (HTTP & versions, QUIC), 1.5.2 (C10K) and 1.5.4 (the 3-minute URL narrative). SD teaches *what
to choose*; this phase teaches *how it works on the wire and in the kernel*: header fields, state machines,
RTT estimators, cwnd traces, socket-level failure modes. TLS depth is owned by Phase 13.4.
**Reading:** K&R 1–3; TCPIP 10–17; RFC 9293 (TCP); RFC 9000 (QUIC); Jacobson 1988; Cardwell 2016.

# Phase 11 — Databases II: Internals

The CMU 15-445 arc, bottom-up: disk-oriented storage and page layout, buffer pools, hash and B+ tree indexes
(and their concurrency), LSM trees, sorting and join algorithms, query execution and optimisation,
concurrency-control theory, 2PL, timestamp ordering, OCC and MVCC, logging and ARIES recovery, and a pointer
into distributed databases. Three build labs (buffer pool, B+ tree, WAL-backed KV store) make the mechanisms
concrete.
**SD overlap map:** SD 2.2 (indexes & an EXPLAIN lab focused on index choice), 2.3 (pages/WAL/buffer pool,
LSM, B-tree vs LSM, row vs column at design level), 2.4 (ACID, isolation levels, anomalies & a lab), 2.5 (locks,
MVCC, optimistic vs pessimistic), 2.6–2.7 (replication, sharding), 2.8 (NoSQL), 3.4–3.5 (quorums, consensus),
5.5.1 (2PC), 7.2.1 (OLTP vs OLAP). SD decides *which* engine and isolation level to use; this phase builds the
engine: split/merge algorithms, cost formulas, lock protocols with proofs, ARIES passes. Distributed data
design stays owned by SD (11.5.4 is a pointer, not a duplicate).
**Reading:** CMU #03–#23; Petrov *Database Internals* (DBI) 1–7, 13; Hellerstein, Stonebraker & Hamilton 2007;
Mohan 1992 (ARIES); Berenson et al. 1995; Cahill 2008; Crotty et al. 2022; Selinger 1979; Leis 2015; O'Neil 1996.
